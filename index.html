    Ethics & Responsibility

    Responsible AI Considerations

    🔍Transparency

    Every classification comes with an explanation of why the item was placed in that category, so users always understand the reasoning.

    ⚖️Fairness

    Classification is based solely on the nature of the waste item. No personal characteristics, identity, or demographics influence the result.

    🔒Privacy

    No personal information is required or collected. Only the waste item text and classification result are saved locally in your browser — nothing is transmitted externally.

    🛡️Safety

    Hazardous waste items are flagged explicitly with guidance to consult certified local disposal facilities — the system does not encourage unsafe disposal practices.

    📊Limitation

    This prototype uses keyword matching against a curated knowledge base. It may be uncertain for unfamiliar, ambiguous, or highly specific items not in the database.

    📍Local Verification

    Waste regulations vary by city and country. Users should always verify classifications against their local municipal waste-management guidelines.

    Sustainability

    Sustainability Impact

    • 🌱 Composting & Organic Waste: Properly segregating wet/organic waste enables composting, returning nutrients to the soil and reducing the volume of waste sent to landfill.
    • ♻️ Recycling: Separating dry recyclable materials such as paper, plastic, and metal allows these to be processed into new products, conserving raw materials and reducing manufacturing energy use.
    • 📱 Responsible E-Waste Handling: Directing electronic waste to authorised recyclers prevents toxic substances from entering the environment and enables recovery of valuable metals.
    • ⚠️ Safer Hazardous Waste Disposal: Correct identification and disposal of hazardous items protects water sources, soil, and communities from chemical contamination.
    • 💡 Responsible Consumption: Awareness of how waste is classified encourages mindful purchasing — choosing products that are recyclable, repairable, or have minimal hazardous components.
